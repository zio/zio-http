In history and bookmarks the same icon repeats down the list, and people often strip bookmark names to save space; the tile is still the darkest, most saturated shape in the row. On a phone the home-screen icon is the full-bleed square, rounded by iOS. “ZIO HTTP” is eight characters and fits under the icon, so short_name can be the full name. Avatars take the maskable square, which GitHub crops to a rounded square and chat apps to a circle. The ZIO HTTP repository lives under the zio organisation, so this avatar is for accounts and servers of its own. Slack and Discord draw the side bar of a link preview in the page's theme-color: mint. The preview texts are the first lines of the Endpoint and Routes reference pages.

Before release, check
Light and dark tab strip, active and inactive tab
20 or more tabs open: icon only, still easy to find
Pinned tab and a bookmark without a name
1× and 2× screens (the 16 and 32 px sizes in favicon.ico)
A browser without SVG favicons falls back to favicon.ico
If favicon-adaptive.svg ships: switch the OS scheme and reload; the arrows change colour
iOS home screen: apple-touch-icon.png is a full-bleed square
Android: the maskable icon keeps the arrows inside the safe circle
Slack and Discord previews show the mint side bar and the og:image
Today ziohttp.com links favicon.ico (the tile at 16, 32 and 48 px) and a 180 px apple-touch-icon from docusaurus.config.js. The SVG link, the manifest and theme-color are new (proposal): the kit's SVGs in website/brand/icons are reference only and have to be copied to website/static/img, and icon-maskable-512.png exported from its SVG. The SVG link comes first; browsers that read SVG favicons take it, the rest fall back to the ICO. theme-color is mint as the social cards set it, so link previews get a mint side bar; Safari and Chrome on Android also tint their toolbar with it.
