Brand sheet · 08 · Social cards

One grid, four templates

Every card follows one grid: the wordmark at the top left, a mono eyebrow, the title, and a mono meta line, all inside an 80 px safe zone. The mark repeats as a tone-on-tone watermark bleeding off the right edge, so a ZIO HTTP card is recognisable in a feed before the title is read. Cards are drawn at full size and shown scaled; the copy is for the maintainers to confirm.

80 px safe zone. All type sits inside it. X rounds the corners, and Slack and LinkedIn crop to slightly different ratios.
Only the title grows. Past two lines, shorten the copy rather than shrinking the type: at feed size the title has to read at about 30 px.
Watermark, tone on tone. The mark at 62% of the card width, 14% of it past the right edge, centred on the height. It stays under 1.2:1 against its ground (1.09:1 on navy, 1.10:1 on the page colour, 1.17:1 on mint, 1.13:1 on paper, 1.12:1 on white), so it never competes with the title.
Sizes at 1200 × 630. Wordmark 38 px, eyebrow and meta 22 px JetBrains Mono, title 72 px Inter Tight 600 at −0.035em, code chip 24 px.

8.5
Rules
Wordmark, never mark plus name. Every card opens with the wordmark; the arrows are the Z. The tile is for avatars and never sits beside “ZIO HTTP”.
Same grid everywhere. 80 px margins, wordmark top left, eyebrow, title and meta along the bottom. The margin is the safe area, so nothing goes nearer an edge.
Export at 2×. PNG for the platforms, dark and light where both exist. The SVG sources convert the wordmark to outlines, so a card never depends on a font being installed.
Copy is a sample. Titles, the release number and the talk are placeholders; take them from the page, the release notes or the event.
