Brand sheet · 02 · Icon and favicon

The tile reads on every tab

The icon is the mark on a navy tile, drawn on the same 32-unit grid as the logo. Mint arrows on navy hold 10.71:1 whatever is behind them, so one tile serves the light tab, the dark tab, the app icon and the avatar. The bare arrows ship too, one colour per scheme, for grounds you control.

Tile on the dark page
Navy on the void ground is 1.06:1, so the tile edge disappears. The mint arrows still read at 10.71:1, which is all the tile has to do.

Full-bleed navy square for Android and PWA masks. The mark is drawn at 60 % (307 of 512 px); its farthest stroke edge is 132 px from the centre, inside the 205 px safe circle (dashed).

2.2
Favicon
The favicon is the logo mark, not a simplified copy: the same four strokes on the 32-unit grid. At 16 px one unit is half a pixel, so the stroke is 1.5 px, the bars sit 5 px apart and both arrowheads stay open at 1×. At 32 px, the size on 2× screens, the stroke is 3 px.
The tile is the default favicon: one file for light and dark tabs. The bare arrows ship in one colour per scheme and need the browser to pick the right file.

The tile stands out on a light strip (navy on grey 14.44:1); on a dark strip its edge fades (1.57:1) and it reads as mint arrows, which is the dark-mode look anyway. The bare mark is lighter in the strip but only right when the scheme matches the tab: a custom browser theme can put deep mint on a dark tab (2.25:1) or mint on a white one (1.77:1), both under the 3:1 that icons need. That risk is why the tile is the default (proposal).

Upload the maskable square as a PNG. Chat apps and X crop it to a circle, GitHub organisations to a rounded square. The farthest stroke edge is 132 px from the centre of the 512 px square, against a 256 px circle radius, so neither crop touches an arrowhead.
2.5
One-colour versions
favicon-mono.svg strokes with currentColor, so it takes the colour of the text around it. For single-ink print, embossing and stamps: navy on white (18.93:1), white on navy, or navy on a mint ground (10.71:1). Both arrows always share the colour. Never mint on white (1.77:1).
